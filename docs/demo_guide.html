Part 1: Demo Video Production Workflow

1. Screen Setup & Perspectives

  • Resident View (Main Window): Open / (Full dashboard). This is the deaf resident’s tablet/laptop showing the arrival pulse, high-contrast risk bands, architectural seam (bilingual cards), and suggested replies.
  • Door Display (Visitor View): Open /door on an iPad or side-by-side window. Click "Tap to turn on sound" so Amazon Polly speaks aloud when replies are sent.
  • Scenario Controller (Off-camera): Open /demo on a phone or separate monitor to trigger scenarios with one tap.

2. Pre-Recording Stack Warmup (2 Mins Before Recording)

Run the automated warmup script in PowerShell to prime AWS Bedrock Claude 3.5 Haiku and AWS Lambda:

.\tasks.ps1 demo --deployed
Note: Wait 45 seconds after warmup so Amazon Bedrock's per-minute quota resets cleanly.

3. Recording Guidelines

  • Target Duration: 2:40 – 2:55 minutes (strictly under the 3:00 hackathon cutoff).
  • Audio Capture: Record both microphone voiceover and system audio so Amazon Polly's voice from /door is clearly audible.
  • Authenticity Rule: Do not speed up card arrivals artificially; the timings panel proves real sub-3-second serverless latency.

Part 3: Quick Troubleshooting While Recording

  • Browser blocks Polly audio: Always tap "Tap to turn on sound" on /door before the recording starts.
  • Old visits clogging feed: Run .\tasks.ps1 demo --deployed to reset history to a clean state.
  • Bedrock throttling: If a safety check takes over 5s, leave 20–30s pause between triggering Scenario 2 and Scenario 3.
